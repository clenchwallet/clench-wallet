Security reports

If a report includes exploitable details, private keys, seed phrases, logs with sensitive data, or a working proof of concept, do not post it publicly. Send it to cw@clench.net.

Lost seeds and passphrases

Clench cannot recover lost recovery phrases, passphrases, private keys, or hardware-wallet secrets. Keep backups offline and test recovery before relying on a wallet.
