Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. Clench Wallet does not use accounts, analytics, telemetry, cookies, or third-party tracking scripts.

Summary

Clench is a non-custodial Bitcoin wallet for Android. The app does not collect personal information, and Clench does not operate an account or recovery service.

What Clench does not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, account identifiers, or advertising identifiers.
  • No app telemetry, usage analytics, or crash reports sent to Clench.
  • No cookies, analytics tags, tracking pixels, forms, or third-party scripts on this website.
  • No wallet balances, addresses, recovery phrases, passphrases, private keys, or watch-only wallet data sent to Clench-operated servers.

Wallet data

Wallet data is stored on your device. Seed phrases, private keys, passphrases, and hardware-wallet secrets are not sent to Clench. When you use a hardware wallet, Clench moves the unsigned transaction to the signer and brings the signed transaction back for review and broadcast.

Clench cannot recover lost recovery phrases, passphrases, private keys, or hardware-wallet secrets.

Network connections

The app connects to Bitcoin infrastructure based on your settings:

  • The Electrum server you select for wallet synchronization and transaction broadcast.
  • Optional price services when fiat value display is enabled.
  • Optional mempool.space or a compatible server for fee and transaction information.
  • Tor through a SOCKS5 proxy such as Orbot when enabled.

Using a public server can reveal wallet queries to that server. Tor can hide your IP address from the server, but it does not make address reuse or poor coin selection private.

This website

Clench.net serves HTML, CSS, and image files. It does not set cookies, run analytics, load third-party scripts, or collect form submissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cw@clench.net.
