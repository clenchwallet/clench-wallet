Adds direct TAPSIGNER signing for single-signature BIP84 native-SegWit payments, with complete review, returned-signature verification, and explicit broadcast. Improves real-card CBOR, derive, network, and xpub compatibility; adds numeric-first PIN entry with a legacy fallback. Also upgrades to BDK Android 3.0 with persisted-wallet migration proof, verifies Room/SQLCipher on Android, and updates the Android/Kotlin toolchain.
