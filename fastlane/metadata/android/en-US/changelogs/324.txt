Security hardening release:

- Uses explicit Android OS entropy for new Clench seeds and fails closed if secure randomness is unavailable.
- Rejects external hardware-wallet signatures that do not commit to the full reviewed transaction.
- Keeps seed creation and verification protected from screenshots, screen recording, Recents previews, and obscured touches.
- Strengthens PIN/device authentication, Electrum TLS and Tor routing, backups, and hostile QR/NFC/file imports.
- Strengthens TAPSIGNER setup checks and tightens release-key isolation, reproducible-build evidence, and dependency auditing.

Physical hardware tests remain separately documented. A direct NFC tap is a trusted physical boundary, and direct TAPSIGNER payment signing is not yet implemented.
