authorized only through the protected release workflow after every required
automated gate passes on the exact protected `master` commit targeted by the
pinned-key signed tag.

## Ship decision

`AUTHORIZED TO SHIP` — publish v0.3.27 only after the pull-request head and
exact protected `master` pass their required checks and a new pinned-key signed
`v0.3.27` tag targets that exact commit. Any version, tag, package, signer,
dependency, migration, test, checksum, SBOM, provenance, unsigned-build, or
full-entry comparison failure cancels publication. Do not upload an APK
manually or weaken the release controls to accommodate a mismatch.

The maintainer's explicit authorization accepts the documented physical-test
deferral. It does not expand TAPSIGNER beyond the supported BIP-84
single-signature scope or turn the debug-candidate result into evidence for the
final signed artifact.
